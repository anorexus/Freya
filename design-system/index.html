Rules

Taken from the product plan, so the interface does not fight the sheet contract.

Color means money

Income is green.500, expense is deepOrange.500, and red.500 appears only when a budget is already broken. Everyday expense amounts stay near-white so a normal month does not look like an alarm.

Amounts stay positive

The sheet stores a positive amount and a type. On a receipt, the amount carries the type: income is green and prefixed with +, expense stays near-white with no minus. The word Income or Expense stays on the sheet table. Transfer never enters the income or expense totals.

Receipts roll up

History groups rows that share a transaction_id. The closed row shows the summed total and the L1 summary. SKU lines, L2 categories, and item names appear only when the receipt is open.

Two kinds of bad data

A dirty cell (bad amount or date) is a warning, amber.500. That row drops out of totals and writes stay open. A broken header or an extra tab is a block: the banner uses red.500 and write actions disable until the sheet matches the contract.

Selection is tonal

The active nav item is a lighter surface with white text, the same move as the references. It does not get a brand color.

Primitives

The 2014 Material Design palettes, same hex values as @mui/material/colors. Shade 500 is the main swatch. Accent shades are A100, A200, A400, and A700.

Type

Inter, from Google Fonts. Every figure uses tabular numbers so columns of rupiah line up. Stat figures drop the “Rp” prefix; the label carries the unit.
